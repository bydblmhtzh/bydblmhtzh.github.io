BSDF 论文跟读：GGX 与透射项

BSDF，首先区分f_s这个是其他scatter散射，并非BRDF的specular。
$$
f_s(i,o,n) = f_r(i,o,n)+f_t(i,o,n)
$$其中：$$f_r(i,o,n) = \frac{F(i,h_r)F(i,h_r)F(i,h_r)}{
4|i\cdot n||o\cdot n|
}$$
论文定义了一个transmission(BTDF)下的half向量$$\begin{gathered}h_t = -(\eta_ii+\eta_oo)\
f_t = \frac{|i\cdot h_t||o\cdot h_t|}{|i\cdot n||o\cdot n|}\frac{\eta_o^2(1-F(i,h_t))G(i,o,h_t)D(h_t)}{
(\eta_i(i\cdot h_t)+\eta_o(o\cdot h_t))^2
}
\end{gathered}$$
$$
c = |\mathbf{i} \cdot \mathbf{n}|
$$

$$
g = \sqrt{\frac{\eta_t^2}{\eta_i^2} - 1 + c^2}
$$

$$
F(i,n) = \frac{1}{2}
\frac{(g - c)^2}{(g + c)^2}
\left(
1 + \frac{(c(g + c) + 1)^2}{(c(g - c) - 1)^2}
\right)
$$

全反射时 $F = 1$

$$
G(i, o, n) = G_1(i, n) ; G_1(o, n)
$$
图2 — GGX D + Smith G₁
$$
D(m) = \frac{\alpha_g^2 ; \chi^+(m \cdot n)}{\pi \cos^4\theta_m ; (\alpha_g^2 + \tan^2\theta_m)^2}
$$

$$
G_1(v, m) = \chi^+\left( \frac{v \cdot m}{v \cdot n} \right)
\frac{2}{1 + \sqrt{1 + \alpha_g^2 \tan^2\theta_v}}
$$
